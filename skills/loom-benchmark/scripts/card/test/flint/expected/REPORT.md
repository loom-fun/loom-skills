How the values on this repository's model card were measured. Generated from `info.json` (schema `loom-card/2`) by the loom-skills card generator; the card's README.md is rendered from the same data.

- Package: this repository. Programs generated by loom-skills `af9f2a0` with hrx-loom `0.1.0` (hrx-system `d01d1c1`).
- Checkpoint: [ggml-org/Flint-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/Flint-GGUF) @ `468307a1b38259e9e4c9d4fe53d4e3b76386572c`, `Flint-Q4_K_M.gguf`, sha256 `c3c4d93f9db92627a2fc47ea8fe8c64a094cabd3549db544bcdd2c00e9a9a347`.
- Drafter: [incoai/Flint-DFlash2-GGUF](https://huggingface.co/incoai/Flint-DFlash2-GGUF) @ `74e6ac0c538cfd393d97bd93694e5c4c6b6aa8c3`, `Flint-DFlash2-Q4_K_M.gguf`; Loom speculative decoding on (DFlash, depth 3).
- Baseline: llama.cpp `llamacpp-rocm/b1355`, server arguments `-ngl 99 -cram 0 --spec-type draft-dflash --spec-draft-model ~/drafter.gguf --spec-draft-n-max 3 -ngld 99 -c 65536 -kvu -np 4` (model path, host and port left out).
- Programs measured: the sha256 below is card_lib.programs_sha256, over `program/*.loom` without each file's first-line generator comment.

## Method

- `perf.py` (loom-benchmark) sends real-text requests: a short prompt for decode, decode after 2k and 32k tokens of context, and prefill of documents of about 1k, 2k, 8k, 16k and 32k tokens. Each run gives one value per metric; the card shows the mean of the runs.
- `c4_steady.py` runs 4 concurrent streams and reports the aggregate decode rate over the window in which all 4 are decoding; the card shows the median of the runs.
- One engine runs at a time on the same machine, with the same GGUF. Loom rates are timed by the client; llama.cpp rates come from its server-side `timings`.
- Drafted tokens accepted is the `spec` gate's count: accepted over proposed draft tokens across its requests.
- Gates are loom-benchmark's `gates.sh` at the LEVEL shown; DevLab runs are the nightly-gates workflow on AMD DevLab Dispatch.
